# Research Dossier — Unassumed waitlist lead magnets

## Stage 0: Research Frame

**Topic and decision.** Which 3–4 downloadable PDF "freebies" (lead magnets) should Unassumed build *now* to maximise qualified waitlist signups from first-time founders before/around the Nov 2026 private beta, and what should each contain so it (a) converts a visitor into a signup, (b) gets used, and (c) pre-sells the product's method (assumptions → rehearsal → evidence) without contradicting the brand promise "no spam, no drip, no validation theatre".

**Context (inferred from repo, not asked):** solo/small founder team (hAIlander studio, London); audience = first-time, pre-product founders, English-speaking, UK/EU/US; horizon = next 4–8 weeks; constraints = must be quick to make, PDF, on-brand (ink/paper/yellow/teal, Archivo/JetBrains Mono/Shantell Sans), honest (no invented stats or testimonials), delivered via the one confirmation email (Resend) — not a drip sequence; GDPR/UK-GDPR compliant consent.

**Definitions.**
- *Lead magnet*: a free asset exchanged for an email address.
- *Qualified signup*: someone who matches the ICP (first-time founder, pre-PMF, hasn't run ≥10 customer interviews) and is plausibly a beta user/payer.
- *Actionable*: the reader completes a concrete output (filled sheet, rewritten question, ranked assumption) in < 30 min.
- *Pre-selling the method*: the PDF lets the reader experience the problem Unassumed solves (bad questions, untested assumptions) and makes the paid product the obvious next step.
- *Assumption / evidence*: as defined on the site — belief with a status vs. what a real person did or said.

**Sub-questions.**
1. What makes a lead magnet convert for *this* audience (format, specificity, length, promise)?
2. Which founder jobs-to-be-done are most painful and most adjacent to Unassumed's paid value (interviewing, assumption mapping, canvas, experiments, AI prompts)?
3. What already exists free and well-known (Mom Test summaries, Strategyzer canvas, YC guides), and where is the gap so ours isn't a commodity?
4. Does a freebie cannibalise the product (giving away the method) or prime demand?
5. What delivery/consent mechanics fit a "no drip" promise and UK GDPR?

**Expert lineup.** Practitioner (growth/lead-gen marketer for early-stage SaaS), Skeptic, Economist, Historian, Academic. **Swap-in:** add an *Affected party — first-time founder* voice (the reader), because no default role represents the person who must actually download and use the PDF.

**Claims to test (Skeptic targets).**
- C1. "Checklists/templates are the highest-converting lead magnet format."
- C2. "Gating content behind email produces higher-quality leads than ungated content."
- C3. "Giving away your method for free cannibalises the paid product."
- C4. "Founders want a Business Model Canvas guide" (vs. the canvas being a commodity they already have).
