THE QUESTION THAT RUINS EVERYTHING

Compliments feel useful. Only past behaviour is.

BAD QUESTION Would you use this?

Asks a stranger to predict their own future behaviour, and hands them the polite answer for free. You will hear yes. You will learn nothing.

BETTER QUESTION Tell me about the last time this happened.

Asks for a memory instead of a forecast. You get a date, a workaround, a cost and a person: the raw material of a real business.
